# Simple welcome doodle

Current asset: `public/illustrations/adithi-doodle.png`

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The earlier detailed journaling portrait is retained as an unused asset.

## Initial generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: tiny original hand-drawn avatar for a personal portfolio. Draw an extremely simple generic black-ink doodle of a young woman’s head and shoulders, medium-length black hair with a center part and ends just past her shoulders. A loose rounded face outline, two tiny dot eyes, two short straight eyebrow strokes, a small curved smile. NO realistic portrait detail. One small simplified bow pendant may be shown as just two loops on a curved necklace line. Use a handful of confident but imperfect felt-tip pen strokes, uneven hand pressure, slightly wobbly curves and imperfect proportions like somebody quickly doodled it in the corner of a notebook in 20 seconds. White negative space in face, solid black hair with two or three white gaps. Friendly, understated, very minimal. NOT a polished vector icon, NOT a mascot logo, NOT manga, NOT fashion illustration, NOT watercolor, NO shading, NO skin color, NO eyelashes, NO rosy cheeks, NO scenery, NO desk, NO laptop, NO lettering, NO frame. Original design, do not depict the character from uthinhpham.com. Single centered small bust with generous padding on a truly transparent background. The silhouette should read clearly at 100px.

## Refinement prompt

Refine this tiny hand-ink doodle avatar. Keep the same very simple face, black shoulder-length hair, dot eyes, smile and tiny bow necklace. Remove ALL grey shading, grey smudges, and drop shadow below the shoulders. End the shoulders in a simple curved black ink line; outside that line must be fully transparent. No grey underside, no floating base or cutout shadow. Keep warm imperfect black marker lines, white face, absolutely no additional detail. Crop composition closer with about 8% clear transparent margin around the head-and-shoulders figure so it reads well at 140px. Retain genuine alpha transparency.
